## Hey! Listen! Meet Navi
Navi schedules our rehearsals and sends reminders. Here's how it works:

**Voting on rehearsal polls**
When Navi posts a poll, react with the number of **every** time that works for you (or 👍 if there's only one time). Voting closes after 24 hours, and Navi posts the winning time in the same channel.

**Reminders**
If you voted for the winning time, Navi DMs you **2 days before** and at **9 AM on rehearsal day**. If your DMs are closed, Navi mentions you in the channel instead.

**Commands**
`/navi rehearsal`: start a rehearsal poll. Pick up to 10 days, choose a time for each, then hit **Send poll**. If there's a tie or fewer than 4 votes, Navi DMs you to decide the final outcome.
`/navi next`: shows the next rehearsal, how many people are on the reminder list, and whether you're one of them. It also shows any poll that's still open.
`/navi rsvp`: missed the poll? This adds you to the reminder list for the next rehearsal.
